# Project Statement

## Project Title

Campus Eats

## Problem Statement

The students on campus might have to go through various outlets and inquire about the food items and prices before ordering their desired meals.

Campus Eats is a simple console application that offers a solution by providing an option of viewing the list of campus cafes and their menu, adding items to the cart, managing the cart and proceeding to billing through one Python program.

## Scope of the Project

The project revolves around the creation of a simple campus food ordering program on Python.

The program would offer the following:

* View available campus cafes
* Browse food items and prices
* Add items to a cart
* Remove items from the cart
* Update item quantities
* View the cart and total amount
* Place an order
* Generate and display a bill
* View order history during the current program session

The project is a console-based application that does not offer online payment options, account management or a permanent order tracking system.

## Target Users

The main target users of the program are:

* College students
* Students ordering from the cafes
* Campus users who would like an option to browse the menu and manage their orders

## High-Level Features

The following are the main features of the Campus Eats application:

* Cafe and menu view
* Cart management
* Billing
* Order placement
* Order history view
